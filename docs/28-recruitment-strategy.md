This is a recruitment strategy, not a promise that any role, market, compensation, schedule, classification, or benefit exists. Those facts must come from an approved job record.

### Current recruitment state (September 19, 2026)

The recruitment model is now active, not evergreen only. The approved facts are recorded in `01-business-source-of-truth.md` section 33 and summarized here:

- Several current Mobile GPS/ELD/AOBRD Installation Technician openings exist, applications are accepted nationwide, and the current openings have no closing date.
- The role is for independent contractors, not employees.
- Starting compensation is `$1,600 per week`, presented as a starting rate for the current opening and never as guaranteed weekly income.
- Approximately 75% travel and state-to-state travel are required. Contractors provide their own personal vehicle, tools, and travel resources. A valid driver's license, a smartphone for installation data submission and installation photos, and a background check are required.
- No prior installation experience is required, and training will be provided.
- MECP and the listed platform experience may be shown as preferred applicant experience only.

Four journeys stay separate in routes, forms, data, analytics events, and confirmation language:

| Journey | Purpose | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Active job application | Apply to one specific active opening | Uses "Apply". Requires that opening's own record, canonical URL, and working application path |
| Installer-network registration | Register interest for future opportunities | Not an application. Uses "Join the Installer Network". Guarantees nothing |
| Commercial inquiry | Request installation service | Never used by candidates |
| Referral inquiry | Its own pathway | Never merged with applications, network registration, or commercial inquiries. Its route, form, and confirmation are not defined here |

Site roles: the homepage remains commercial-led. The Careers pages are the primary recruitment funnel.

The header's secondary objective, maintaining an installer network between active hiring cycles, is read together with this section: the network now operates for future opportunities alongside the active openings, not only between hiring cycles.

Build status (September 19, 2026): the job record, opening page, jobs index, application page, application form, and confirmation page exist. The form's submission path depends on the forms endpoint, which is not verified, so treat the application as built but unproven end to end. "Apply" belongs only to the active opening's routes.

## 2. Recruitment Outcomes

The program should:

1. increase qualified applications for active openings;
2. help candidates self-qualify before starting an application;
3. reduce confusion between an active job and an evergreen role;
4. capture installer interest for future opportunities when no active opening fits;
5. create consistent job information across the site and recruitment platforms;
6. give recruiters useful source and funnel data;
7. improve candidate experience and follow-up reliability; and
8. build a repeatable system that can scale with verified demand.

Traffic without qualified candidates is not the primary success measure.

---

## 3. Recruitment Operating Principles

- Business truth controls published job facts.
- Every active job has one canonical source page.
- Evergreen recruiting education is not an active vacancy.
- Installer-network registration is not an application.
- Commercial inquiries are a separate journey.
- Referral inquiries are a separate journey.
- Job platforms must match the canonical page.
- Requirements are transparent before the form.
- Candidate data is collected only when needed.
- Follow-up expectations are truthful and approved.
- Closed jobs are updated across every channel.
- Automation supports people; it does not make hiring decisions.

## 4. Candidate Segments

### Experienced mobile installers

May already understand vehicle, fleet, telematics, tracking, electrical, accessory, or equipment installation. They need clear territory, work model, tools, travel, documentation, and assignment expectations.

### Adjacent technical workers

May come from automotive electronics, low-voltage, telecommunications, field service, security, GPS, ELD, or related work. They need a credible explanation of transferable skills and training or onboarding realities where verified.

### Independent installers

May prefer project-based or assignment-based work. They need clarity about engagement structure, availability, travel, documentation, payment, and the difference between an active opening and network registration.

### Referral candidates

May trust an existing installer or professional contact more than a job advertisement. They need a simple, trackable path that does not expose the referrer's or candidate's private information.

### Commercially adjacent visitors

May arrive for services or project questions rather than employment. They should see a discreet careers path without being pushed into an irrelevant candidate form.

## 5. Candidate Decision Questions

Recruitment content should answer, in an accessible order:

- What type of installer work is involved?
- Is there an active opening?
- Where and how is the work performed?
- What experience or skills are required?
- What tools, vehicle, travel, documentation, or schedule expectations apply?
- Is this an employee, contractor, assignment, or other engagement?
- What happens after applying?
- What information will be requested?
- How will the information be used?
- What can a candidate do if no current opening fits?

Unknown facts should be labeled as unconfirmed or omitted, not filled with generic promises.

---

## 6. Employer and Opportunity Positioning

Position the opportunity around verified realities of the work:

- practical technical installation;
- field-based assignments when applicable;
- accurate equipment and documentation expectations;
- clear communication and reliability;
- customer or site professionalism when relevant;
- the type of technical problem the installer helps solve; and
- a transparent path to review.

Avoid unsupported claims about culture, flexibility, income, career advancement, benefits, training, volume of work, or guaranteed assignments.

---

## 7. Recruitment Funnel

Recommended funnel:

1. awareness through search, job platforms, referrals, social, or outreach;
2. landing on a canonical careers, role, market, or job page;
3. self-qualification through facts, requirements, and expectations;
4. active application or installer-network registration;
5. trusted confirmation;
6. human review and follow-up;
7. screening and operational qualification; and
8. assignment, onboarding, or other approved next stage.

The website measures the steps it controls. It does not claim a hiring outcome from an application event.

## 9. Active Job Strategy

Every active job record should define:

- public title;
- job status;
- canonical URL;
- recruiting market or territory;
- location model;
- engagement classification when approved;
- responsibilities;
- required qualifications;
- preferred qualifications;
- tools, vehicle, travel, or documentation expectations;
- schedule information when approved;
- compensation information when approved or required;
- application destination;
- publication date;
- closing or review date when applicable;
- source attribution; and
- owner responsible for updates.

Only validated active records may produce active job pages, platform postings, JobPosting schema, or urgent CTA language.

---

## 10. Evergreen Recruiting Strategy

Evergreen pages support discovery and preparation alongside active openings, and they are the fallback when no opening fits. They may explain:

- what mobile installers do;
- related installer role categories;
- transferable skills;
- typical field expectations when verified;
- tools and documentation;
- assignment concepts;
- application preparation;
- candidate FAQs; and
- installer-network registration.

Evergreen pages must clearly state that role information is educational or general when no active opening is linked.

---

## 11. Installer Network Strategy

The installer network is a registration channel for future opportunities, offered alongside the active openings, for qualified or potentially qualified installers. It is not the application for a current opening. A candidate who wants a current opening uses that opening's own application path once it exists.

### Network proposition

Explain:

- who should register;
- what information may be requested;
- what types of opportunities may be communicated;
- how information may be used;
- whether registration creates any obligation;
- that registration does not guarantee work, assignment, interview, or employment; and
- how a person can update or withdraw information.

### Network conversion

Use a separate form, endpoint, confirmation, analytics event, and operational queue. Never count a network registration as an application.

---

## 12. Recruitment Platform Strategy

Use the website as the canonical source and recruitment platforms as distribution channels.

For every posting:

- use the approved public job title;
- match location and work model;
- match requirements and responsibilities;
- link to the canonical page or approved ATS destination;
- use consistent application language;
- include source attribution;
- update or close the posting when the job changes; and
- keep the website page, platform copy, and internal job record aligned.

Detailed channel procedures are in 29-recruitment-platform-distribution.md.

## 13. Channel Mix

Potential channels, subject to approval and audience fit:

- Indeed or comparable job boards;
- LinkedIn Jobs and organic LinkedIn;
- ZipRecruiter or comparable distribution;
- Google for Jobs eligibility through structured data;
- trade, automotive, electrical, low-voltage, and field-service communities;
- local workforce and trade organizations;
- installer referrals;
- professional networking;
- targeted email outreach where lawful and permission-based;
- social recruiting posts; and
- the company website and Google Business Profile as supporting discovery assets.

Do not publish to every platform automatically. Choose channels based on candidate quality, cost, reach, operational capacity, and data.

---

## 14. Referral Strategy

Create a simple referral route for approved referrers.

Referral program requirements:

- explain who can refer;
- identify any incentive only if approved;
- avoid collecting unnecessary personal data;
- provide candidate privacy notice;
- route the candidate to an active opening or network path;
- tag the referral source;
- prevent duplicate submissions where practical; and
- make no promise that a referral results in hiring.

Referral reporting should separate referral source from candidate outcome.

---

## 15. Outreach Strategy

Recruiter or manager outreach should be specific and respectful.

An outreach brief should include:

- why the person appears relevant;
- the verified role or opportunity;
- location and work model;
- key requirements;
- what information is requested;
- canonical job or network link;
- response or opt-out instructions; and
- sender identity.

Do not use scraped personal data, misleading subject lines, or messages that imply a guaranteed opportunity.

---

## 16. Content and SEO Recruitment Program

Prioritize content that maps to candidate questions:

- mobile installer jobs;
- vehicle and fleet installation roles;
- GPS, telematics, or ELD installation when verified;
- field installer requirements;
- installer tools and experience;
- travel and assignment expectations;
- how to prepare for an installer application;
- active market hiring pages; and
- network registration questions.

Every article or guide should connect to a relevant role, opening, requirements page, or network path without implying that an opening is active.

---

## 17. Local Recruitment

Local recruiting pages may target an approved hiring market, but they must not pretend the company has a physical office or guaranteed local work unless verified.

Use:

- accurate market naming;
- real territory or work-model explanation;
- local job-platform distribution;
- workforce and trade relationships;
- locally relevant candidate questions; and
- consistent contact and entity data.

Do not create doorway pages with minor city-name substitutions.

---

## 18. Candidate Experience Standards

Candidates should receive:

- clear status and requirements;
- a mobile-friendly path;
- plain-language form instructions;
- meaningful error recovery;
- realistic confirmation copy;
- clear privacy explanation;
- no forced commercial inquiry;
- no duplicated application request without explanation; and
- a useful alternative when an opening closes.

Do not make candidates search the site to discover whether an opening is active.

---

## 19. Screening and Handoff

The website may collect and route information. Screening and hiring decisions remain an approved human or operational process.

Define:

- who reviews submissions;
- review queue;
- duplicate handling;
- minimum screening information;
- follow-up ownership;
- status vocabulary;
- disqualification or hold reasons;
- retention period;
- candidate communication template; and
- escalation for incomplete or suspicious submissions.

Do not let an automated workflow imply acceptance, rejection, or assignment unless an approved human-reviewed process authorizes that message.

- qualified applicant rate;

## 22. Attribution

Use consistent source parameters for:

- website CTA;
- job-board links;
- social posts;
- referrals;
- outreach;
- QR or printed recruiting assets; and
- workforce or trade partnerships.

Attribution should answer which channel produced a visit or submission. It should not be used to infer a hiring outcome without operational data.

## 24. Compliance and Candidate Privacy

- publish only lawful and approved job information;
- avoid discriminatory language;
- distinguish required job qualifications from preferences;
- provide applicant privacy information;
- collect only necessary data;
- restrict access to submissions;
- document retention and deletion;
- use secure submission handling;
- do not put resumes or contact details into analytics; and
- obtain permission for candidate testimonials, images, or stories.

Legal review is required for jurisdiction-specific employment, contractor, pay-transparency, background-check, or privacy requirements.

## 25. Budget and Resourcing

Budget decisions should consider:

- job-board fees;
- recruiter time;
- screening and follow-up capacity;
- referral incentives if approved;
- content production;
- photography or image production;
- ATS or form costs;
- automation costs; and
- analytics and reporting time.

Do not increase media spend while application handling is backlogged or the job information is inaccurate.

## 26. 30/60/90-Day Recruitment Priorities

### First 30 days

- verify job records and platform alignment;
- launch or refine Careers and Current Openings;
- confirm application and network routing;
- baseline source and funnel data;
- publish priority postings;
- collect candidate questions; and
- correct urgent friction.

### Days 31–60

- expand evergreen role and requirements content;
- test one application or CTA improvement;
- activate approved referral and workforce channels;
- refine recruiting-market pages;
- improve platform copy from performance data; and
- document screening handoff.

### Days 61–90

- evaluate source quality and cost;
- improve high-impression, low-conversion pages;
- strengthen network nurturing;
- consolidate weak or duplicate content;
- publish approved candidate resources; and
- set the next quarterly recruitment plan.

---

## 27. Job Closure Procedure

When a job closes:

1. update the central job record;
2. remove or change the active application action;
3. remove JobPosting schema;
4. update the website status;
5. close or update platform postings;
6. update paid or social promotion;
7. offer relevant active openings or network registration;
8. review redirects and sitemap;
9. record the closure date; and
10. verify that no stale cache or campaign still presents the opening as active.

---

## 28. Recruitment Calendar

Maintain a rolling calendar containing:

- hiring-cycle dates;
- job publication and review dates;
- platform refresh dates;
- content publication dates;
- referral and outreach windows;
- workforce or trade events;
- image and creative deadlines;
- reporting dates; and
- job closure and update checkpoints.

Calendar entries need an owner and approved source.

---

## 29. Definition of Done

The recruitment strategy is operational when active job records, canonical pages, platform postings, application and network paths, candidate messaging, screening ownership, privacy controls, attribution, reporting, and closure procedures are documented and tested. The system must distinguish applications from network registrations and must preserve accurate job status across every channel.
